✳ TRANSPARENCY IS A FEATURE

No magic.
Just a model.

This is an interactive thought experiment built around clear, inspectable rules. Here's what happens under the hood.

01 / DATA

Six imagined districts.

Each has a fictional population, hazard vulnerability, and local effectiveness modifiers. The map is deliberately abstract.

02 / DECISIONS

A fixed 100-credit budget.

Invest in trees, drainage, backup microgrids, or community hubs. More investment brings diminishing returns.

03 / OUTPUT

Scores, not predictions.

Weighted neighborhood risk creates the city index. The exposure index is a risk-weighted count, not an estimate of actual people harmed.

04 / TRUST

Nothing leaves your browser.

No accounts, analytics, external APIs or hidden AI. Scenarios save only to your device, with an option to export JSON.

SIMULATION RULE / SIMPLIFIED

District risk = baseline vulnerability − Σ ( √ investment share × crisis weight × district modifier × 41 )

Clamped to a 6–98 score range. City resilience = 100 − population-weighted average district risk. Exact coefficients are readable in model.js.
